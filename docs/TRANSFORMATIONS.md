# Headgear and transformation skills

Buy a transformation item in the Kit shop, then choose an athlete to use it on. One item is consumed to teach a permanent skill; no skill points are charged. If the athlete has an empty skill slot, the new skill is equipped automatically. Otherwise it remains learned until the player makes space. Already-known skills cannot be taught again to that athlete.

| Item | Shop tier | Price | Transformation skill | Equipped bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fox Spirit Mask | 0 | $450 | Fox Spirit | Meet attributes +5% |
| Iron Sentinel Suit | 1 | $1,200 | Iron Sentinel | Meet attributes +8% |
| Owl Scholar Hood | 2 | $1,800 | Owl Scholar | Meet attributes +5%, XP +15% |
| Ember Dragon Suit | 3 | $3,200 | Ember Dragon | Meet attributes +10% |
| Comet Monarch Crown | 4 | $5,500 | Comet Monarch | Meet attributes +12% |

These use normal skill slots and can be changed outside meets. Saved training and meet setups control their own equipped transformations. Boosts from multiple equipped transformations add together, capped at 100 per effective attribute; only the strongest form is drawn (first equipped wins a tie). Base stats, training caps, mastery, athlete identity and unique-character unlocks are unchanged. Owl Scholar's XP bonus applies only in setups where that skill is equipped.

The shop and athlete picker preview forms before purchase. Portraits, training, the club map and meet scenes all use the same appearance selection. The Skills page shows the active training/meet looks, and the Stats page distinguishes effective meet attributes from permanent base stats. These are original masks, suits and character forms rather than copies of another game's characters.
