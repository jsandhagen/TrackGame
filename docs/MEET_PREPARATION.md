# Meet preparation

Training now starts with preparation controls. Choose meet entrants, the whole squad, or the selected training group, then apply a daily plan. Advanced courses show their Insight cost before applying. Unlocking pays once for the club and applies the course to eligible athletes immediately. Injured athletes and manually assigned facility staff are skipped with an explanation; an action with no eligible athletes spends nothing.

Standard auto meet prep is optional and off by default, including for existing saves. While a meet is booked, entrants use all-round training, recover below 40 stamina, and take a full rest day on the calendar day before the meet. Meet day is recovery-only. Injuries still take priority. Bench athletes keep their existing management, and automatic prep never purchases courses, schedules paid sessions or uses items.

This is a basic routine, not a performance optimiser: it does not choose event-specific training blocks, adapt to weather, optimise condition or arrange extra coaching. Manual management retains those advantages. Manual plans, training events, rest/taper settings and staff assignments are preserved. Turning auto prep off restores those choices; applying a group plan or unlocking and assigning a course switches back to manual preparation explicitly.

The routine follows the current meet date and entries, including rescheduled meets and new fixtures. The mode survives saving and loading. Meets and pending club decisions block changes to the mode or group plans.
