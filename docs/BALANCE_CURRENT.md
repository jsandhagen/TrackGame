# Current balance — 0.9.0

Run `AUDIT_SEEDS=6 AUDIT_SEASONS=40 node scripts/balance-audit.mjs`. [Raw results](balance-results-v0.9.0.json). These six-seed scripted samples establish progression paths and regressions, not human enjoyment or a statistically representative win rate.

The engaged policy chooses physical training, skills, upgrades, recruits and equipment. Its purchasing now avoids redundant stock and equips improvements rather than leaving advanced gear in the bag. The fully managed policy also directs secondary-event practice, coaches event delivery, selects developed approaches and assigns unused athletes to automatic economic duties. The idle policy auto-selects lineups and resolves events but never invests. All three enter the next available circuit tournament.

| First World Championship, median season | Not reached within 40 | 24 | Not reached within 40 |
| Careers winning within 40 seasons | 2/6 | 6/6 | 0/6 |

These results include the current street-campus layout and stadium customization changes merged from main. Community, County and Regional remain generous for these highly informed managers. The current resistance primarily occurs at National and World levels; early human playtesting remains necessary. Fifteen seasons remains the theoretical minimum; it is no longer the sample's typical first championship. A previous tuning pass prevented every engaged manager from winning World within 40 seasons and was rejected.

Later rivals have authored skills, specialist equipment, reliable supporting scorers and six-athlete squads, with different weaknesses by club. Their names and development persist across stages and revisits. Attributes are not raised uniformly to create the entire challenge. Recruits now respect their initial development caps.

Practice-only mastery stops at 35 in each event. Competition expands the practice ceiling even after a loss or all-foul series. In a separate 140-day preparation-only seed, the starters reached mastery 35 and displayed ratings 37–39 without playing any meets. [Preparation sample](preparation-results-v0.9.0.json). Open-calendar financial and club development remain available; there is no new financial punishment for waiting.

The tactics regression now distinguishes two questions. Equal-team simulations check the risk profiles of fixed tactics. A separate, paired test runs varied final-throw scoring targets, including athletes without a legal mark, and requires field-aware decisions to outperform every fixed tactic in realised average team-point margin. It does not permit the smart policy to perform worse and call that superiority.

Known limits: generous early tiers, very large late-game treasuries for developed clubs, and rare injuries under automatic recovery. The economy intentionally supports investment-driven snowballing; money alone cannot buy competition experience or remove mastery requirements. Human tests should assess whether later breakthroughs remain satisfying and whether early rivals need additional targeted resistance.
