# Roster facility duties

Squad depth can support the club economy. Assign one roster athlete to each productive building or the Video room. One athlete can hold one duty. Duty assignments repeat automatically as days advance and replace that athlete's daily stat training. They consume stamina and grant a small amount of XP; normal wages remain payable. Tired staff automatically rest, injured athletes recover, and selected meet entrants taper before competition. Removing a duty resumes the saved training plan.

The Clubhouse runs community open days (money and fans); the Canteen and Merch Stand serve visitors (money); the Strength Room provides training partners (Insights); the Sports Lab supports technique testing (Insights); the Video room supports meet-film review (Insights); the Scout Office runs introductory throws clinics (fans); the Workshop services implements and kit (money); Recovery Spa staff support resting teammates (additional recovery).

Revenue-producing visitor services depend on the actual audience and finite service capacity. Upgrading buildings increases productive capacity and rewards. A larger fanbase can sustain a profitable support roster, while a small club must weigh signing costs, wages, building investment, stamina and foregone training. Insight production competes for roster places with commercial services. Basic unstaffed facility effects remain intact.

Assign from Squad → Facility roster, an individual athlete's Daily role selector, or the building window. World animation shows athletes at the assigned facilities with daily currency popups. The UI shows wages and prospective output at today's attendance. Historical staff earnings appear in the facility roster.

Assignments follow moved buildings, clear when a building is sold or an athlete released, and persist in saves. Existing schema-7 saves receive an empty facility roster. Simulation checks cover exclusivity, rest/taper, revenue limits, upgrades, recovery support, persistence and removal.
